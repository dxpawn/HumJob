Progress

Sing or hum a steady note and the graph traces your pitch in real time. Hit Stop and it works out the key of what you sang. Pick a Target key on the circle of fifths and it also tells you how far off you sang it (in cents, or semitones if the gap is a big one). Click the graph (or use Target note) to set a note to practise: the shaded band is the in-tune zone, and the readout shows how steady you are and how long you hold it. Press Play reference note to hear the target for a couple of seconds. Switch to Match game to sing back random notes, Scale trainer to follow a scale up and down in time with a click, or Range for a guided test that measures your maximum range and tessitura, then reports a percentile and a possible voice type. 🎧 Headphones avoid feedback.
